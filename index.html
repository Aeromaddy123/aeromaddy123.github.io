About me

Gears, circuits and gradients.

I’m the engineer who can follow a robot all the way down. I trained in mechanical engineering, went deep into embedded systems, and now work on robot learning, so I can design the gearbox, lay out the circuit, write the firmware and train the policy that drives it.

Along the way I built a bionic arm that’s filed as a patent application, a medical-delivery drone and a five-axis arm. I published first-author research, won six engineering competitions and taught robotics to the next wave of builders. Most recently, at CESI LINEACT with IMT Atlantique, I’ve been teaching a UR16e to handle a kitchen from human demonstrations.

Give me a problem that has to move, and I’ll take it from first sketch to a robot that works.
