UR16e visual geometry and nominal link parameters from Universal Robots.
https://github.com/UniversalRobots/Universal_Robots_ROS2_Description/tree/0eb1a861f7772fceb38510d90fa2f98ec668070c
The UR16e configuration uses the UR10e base, shoulder and wrist meshes, plus the UR16e upper arm and forearm. DAE visual meshes converted to GLB using assimpjs. License included alongside this file.
The marker, lettering, glass and workbench are portfolio illustrations.
